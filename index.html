On your PC

How MicLink works

MicLink sends your iPhone's microphone to your Windows PC over a USB cable or Wi-Fi. Windows sees it as a normal microphone, so you can pick it in Discord, Zoom, OBS and other Windows apps.

The Windows installer sets up the virtual microphone MicLink needs: VB-CABLE, a product of VB-Audio Software, downloaded from VB-Audio during setup. In your apps, choose CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable).

Audio is 48 kHz, 16-bit mono and goes straight from the phone to your PC, over the cable or your local network. It is not sent anywhere else.

The Pause button

Pause without disconnecting

Need your phone for a minute? Pause hands the microphone back to your iPhone. Your PC stays connected and hears silence until you tap Resume, with nothing to do on the PC.

Quick setup

Up and running in three steps

  1. 1

    Step 1: Install MicLink on your PC

    Run the installer. It sets up the virtual microphone MicLink needs and can add Apple's USB support for cable mode.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Start on your iPhone

    Open MicLink, choose USB or Wi-Fi and tap Start. On the free plan, watch a short ad first to get your hour. Then press Connect in MicLink on your PC.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Pick the microphone

    In Discord, Zoom, OBS or Windows itself, choose CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable).

Questions and answers

What do I need?

A PC with Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit) and an iPhone with iOS 17 or later. To connect them you need a USB cable, or both devices on the same Wi-Fi network.

What is the difference between Pause and Mute?

Pause frees your iPhone's microphone, so you can watch a video, take a call or record in another app. Your PC stays connected and hears silence. Tap Resume and you are back, with nothing to do on the PC.

Mute keeps the microphone on and simply sends silence to the PC. It is the quick one, for a cough or a word off the record.

Does it work over both USB and Wi-Fi?

Yes. You choose USB or Wi-Fi in the iPhone app and in MicLink on your PC. Over Wi-Fi, both devices need to be on the same local network.

Which microphone do I choose in Discord, Zoom or OBS?

Choose CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable). That is where your iPhone's audio arrives on Windows. The setup guide shows where the setting is in each app.

What is VB-CABLE, and why does the installer add it?

VB-CABLE is a virtual audio device made by VB-Audio Software. MicLink plays your iPhone's audio into it, and Windows apps see the other end, CABLE Output, as a microphone. The MicLink installer downloads VB-CABLE from VB-Audio during setup, so you do not have to install it yourself.

Windows shows a "Windows protected your PC" message. What now?

The Windows download is not code-signed yet, so Microsoft Defender SmartScreen may show that prompt the first time you open the installer. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

Only do this for the installer you downloaded from this website.

Do I need to install anything from Apple for the USB cable?

Cable mode uses Apple's USB support for iPhone on Windows. The MicLink installer can add it for you during setup. Wi-Fi mode does not need it.

Does it keep working when my iPhone is locked?

Yes. MicLink keeps streaming with the phone locked, and the lock screen and Dynamic Island show Mute, Pause or Resume, and Disconnect buttons while a session is running. Resume on the lock screen needs iOS 18 or later; on iOS 17, open MicLink and tap Resume there.

Can I make my voice louder or quieter?

Yes. The gain slider in the iPhone app goes from 0 to 200 %.

What audio format does MicLink use?

48 kHz, 16-bit, mono. The audio is sent straight from your iPhone to your PC over the cable or your local network.

Does MicLink collect or upload my audio or any data?

Your audio: no. It goes only from your iPhone to your own PC and is never recorded, uploaded or used for ads. There is no account and no MicLink server.

Ads in the free plan: the free plan of the iPhone app shows ads from Google. To show and measure them, Google's ad software collects some device and usage information, such as your IP address (to estimate your general location), a device identifier and which ads you saw. MicLink does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps. MicLink Pro has no ads.

MicLink for Windows collects nothing. The details are in the privacy policy.

Is there a version for Mac or Android?

No. MicLink is an iPhone app with a companion app for Windows 10 and 11.

What does it cost?

MicLink for Windows is free. MicLink for iPhone is free to download, and every feature is in the free plan: you watch a short video ad to get 1 hour of microphone time, as often as you like.

MicLink Pro removes the ads and the time limit. It costs $14.99 per year or $2.99 per month. Those are US prices; they may differ in other countries, and the app shows your price before you buy. The plans are side by side under pricing.

What happens when my free hour runs out?

MicLink tells you a few minutes before. Then the microphone pauses and your PC stays connected, just as if you had tapped Pause. Watch another ad for another hour, or go Pro, and carry on. There is nothing to do on the PC.

Free time only counts while you are streaming to your PC. Muted time counts too, because Mute keeps the microphone on and the stream running; use Pause to stop the clock. You can watch ads ahead of time and save up to 4 hours.

When does the free plan show ads?

Only when you choose to watch one to get microphone time. The ads are videos from Google, and the iPhone needs an internet connection to load one. Your audio still goes only to your own PC.

How do I cancel MicLink Pro?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose MicLink and tap Cancel Subscription. In MicLink, the three dots at the top right and then Manage subscription take you to the same page. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for. After that the app goes back to the free plan.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Apple handles the billing, so refund requests go to Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I subscribed, but the app shows the free plan. How do I restore MicLink Pro?

Check that the iPhone is signed in to the Apple Account you subscribed with. Then tap the three dots at the top right of MicLink and choose Restore Purchases. This also brings Pro to a new or reset iPhone.
