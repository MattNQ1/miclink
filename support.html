Quick answers

Which microphone do I choose in my apps?

Choose CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable) in Discord, Zoom, OBS or the Windows sound settings. The setup guide shows where to find the setting in each app.

What does Pause do?

Pause frees your iPhone's microphone so you can watch a video, take a call or record in another app. Your PC stays connected and hears silence. Tap Resume to come back; there is nothing to do on the PC.

Windows shows a "Windows protected your PC" message

The Windows download is not code-signed yet, so Microsoft Defender SmartScreen may ask. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Only do this for the installer you downloaded from this website.

What do I need to run MicLink?

A PC with Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), an iPhone with iOS 17 or later, and either a USB cable or both devices on the same Wi-Fi network.

What does MicLink cost?

MicLink for Windows is free. MicLink for iPhone is free to download: watch a short video ad to get 1 hour of microphone time, as often as you like. MicLink Pro removes the ads and the time limit for $14.99 per year or $2.99 per month (US prices; they may differ in other countries). See pricing.

How do I cancel MicLink Pro or ask for a refund?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose MicLink and tap Cancel Subscription. In MicLink, the three dots at the top right and then Manage subscription take you to the same page. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for.

Apple handles the billing, so we cannot see your payment or refund it ourselves. Ask Apple for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I subscribed, but the app shows the free plan

Check that the iPhone is signed in to the Apple Account you subscribed with. Then tap the three dots at the top right of MicLink and choose Restore Purchases.

Does MicLink collect anything about me?

Your audio goes only from your iPhone to your own PC and is never recorded, uploaded or used for ads. There is no account and no MicLink server.

The free plan of the iPhone app shows ads from Google, and Google's ad software collects some device and usage information to show and measure them. MicLink Pro has no ads, and MicLink for Windows collects nothing. See the privacy policy.

Fix a problem

The setup guide has step-by-step fixes. Jump straight to the one you need:

  • Wi-Fi: my iPhone is not in the list
  • USB: the PC does not find my iPhone
  • Connected, but the other app hears nothing
  • "CABLE Output" is missing from the microphone list
  • Crackling or short drop-outs
  • The lock screen says "Open MicLink to resume"
  • MicLink paused by itself
  • The ad does not load
