3. The free plan and MicLink Pro

MicLink for Windows is free. MicLink for iPhone is free to download and can be used in two ways.

The free plan

  • You get microphone time in exchange for watching a video ad: at present 1 hour for each ad you watch to the end, with up to 4 hours saved at a time. Time is only used while you are streaming to your PC. That includes time while you are muted; no time is used while you are paused or waiting for the PC.
  • When your free time runs out, the microphone pauses. You can watch another ad or subscribe to MicLink Pro.
  • Ads are provided by Google and need an internet connection on the iPhone. We cannot promise that an ad is available at every moment.
  • Free time has no cash value, cannot be transferred, and is lost if you remove the app.
  • We may change the free plan in a later version of the app, for example how much time an ad gives, or end it. A change to the free plan never shortens a MicLink Pro period you have already paid for.

MicLink Pro

MicLink Pro is an auto-renewable subscription that gives you unlimited microphone time and no ads for as long as it is active. There are two plans:

  • MicLink Pro Yearly: $14.99 per year.
  • MicLink Pro Monthly: $2.99 per month.

These are the prices in the United States. In other countries Apple sets the price in the local currency, and it may include taxes. The app always shows your price before you buy.

Payment and renewal

  • You subscribe inside the iPhone app, through Apple's in-app purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically for the same period, at the price that applies then, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your Apple Account is charged for the renewal within 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • If the price of a plan changes, Apple tells you beforehand and, where its rules require it, asks you to agree before the new price applies to you.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time in your Apple Account settings: on the iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose MicLink and tap Cancel Subscription. After you cancel, MicLink Pro stays active until the end of the period you have paid for and then ends; the app goes back to the free plan. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Refunds

Apple handles all billing for MicLink Pro. We do not take your payment, cannot see it and cannot refund it ourselves, so we give no refunds or credits for a period that has started. To ask for a refund, contact Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com; Apple decides under its own refund policy. Nothing here takes away a right to a refund that the law in your country gives you.

Restoring a subscription

A subscription belongs to the Apple Account that bought it. On a new or reset iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account, tap the three dots at the top right of MicLink and choose Restore Purchases.
