Setup guide

Set up MicLink

Everything from the download to picking the microphone in your apps, for USB and for Wi-Fi.

1. What you need

  • A PC with Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit).
  • An iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
  • A way to connect them: a USB cable that carries data (some cables only charge), or both devices on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • An internet connection on the PC while you install, because the installer downloads VB-CABLE from VB-Audio.

2. Install MicLink on Windows

  1. Download the installer, MicLinkSetup.exe.

    Download for Windows

  2. Open MicLinkSetup.exe. If Windows shows a blue Windows protected your PC window, choose More info, then Run anyway. The download is not code-signed yet, which is why Microsoft Defender SmartScreen asks.
  3. Follow the installer. It sets up the virtual microphone MicLink needs: VB-CABLE, a product of VB-Audio Software, which the installer downloads from VB-Audio during setup. If Windows asks for permission to make changes, choose Yes. If Windows then asks Would you like to install this device software?, that is VB-Audio's driver for the virtual microphone: choose Install.
  4. If you plan to use a USB cable, let the installer add Apple's USB support when it offers to. Wi-Fi does not need it.
  5. If you are asked to restart Windows, restart before you continue. Then open MicLink from the Start menu.

Only choose Run anyway for the installer you downloaded from this website.

3. Install MicLink on your iPhone

MicLink for iPhone needs iOS 17 or later. Price: Free with ads, or Pro from $2.99/month. How the free plan and MicLink Pro work is in section 7.

Coming soon to the App Store

Open MicLink and tap Start. On a new install you have no free time yet, so the plans screen opens first: choose Watch an ad to get 1 hour, or a MicLink Pro plan, and the session starts. The first time a session starts, your iPhone asks for a few permissions:

  • Microphone: choose Allow. MicLink needs it to send your voice to your PC.
  • Local Network (Wi-Fi mode): choose Allow, so your PC can find the iPhone and receive its audio.
  • Live Activities: choose Allow to get the controls on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island.

The first time you watch an ad, and depending on where you live, the app may also show a message from Google that asks how ads may use your data. Choose what you prefer; you can change it later under the three dots at the top right, Ad privacy choices. MicLink does not show Apple's "Allow Tracking" prompt, because it does not ask to track you across other companies' apps.

4. Connect: USB or Wi-Fi

Use whichever suits you. You can switch at any time: set the same mode on the iPhone and on the PC.

With a USB cable

  1. Plug the iPhone into the PC and unlock it. If the iPhone asks, tap Trust.
  2. On the iPhone, set Connection to USB and tap Start.
  3. On the PC, choose USB in MicLink and press Connect.

Over Wi-Fi

  1. Put the iPhone and the PC on the same network, one you trust such as your home Wi-Fi. Guest networks usually do not work, because they keep devices apart.
  2. On the iPhone, set Connection to Wi-Fi and tap Start.
  3. On the PC, choose Wi-Fi in MicLink. Pick your iPhone from the list and press Connect.

iPhone not in the list? Type the address the iPhone shows, for example 192.168.1.20:5555.

The first time you use Wi-Fi, Windows Firewall may ask about MicLink. Allow it on private networks, otherwise the PC cannot find the iPhone.

When it works, the iPhone shows Connected - streaming and the level meter in MicLink on the PC moves when you talk.

5. Pick the microphone in your apps

Your iPhone's audio arrives on Windows as CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable). Choose it wherever you would normally choose a microphone. Menu names can differ slightly between app versions.

Windows

Settings › System › Sound › Input, then choose CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable). Apps that use the Windows default microphone follow this setting.

Discord

User Settings (the gear) › Voice & Video › Input Device, then choose CABLE Output.

Zoom

Settings › Audio › Microphone, then choose CABLE Output. In a meeting, use the arrow next to the microphone button.

OBS Studio

Settings › Audio › Mic/Auxiliary Audio, then choose CABLE Output. Or add an Audio Input Capture source and pick it as the device.

Keep your usual speakers or headphones as the Windows output. Do not choose "CABLE Input" as the output device, or every sound from your PC would be sent into your microphone.

6. Pause, Mute and the lock screen

  • Pause frees the iPhone's microphone so you can watch a video, take a call or record in another app. The PC stays connected and hears silence.
  • Resume turns the microphone back on. There is nothing to do on the PC.
  • Mute keeps the microphone on but sends silence to the PC.
  • Disconnect ends the session. Tap Start to begin a new one.
  • Gain sets how loud you are, from 0 to 200 %.
  • Lock screen and Dynamic Island: while a session is running you get Mute, Pause or Resume, and Disconnect buttons there. MicLink keeps streaming with the phone locked. Resume from there needs iOS 18 or later; on iOS 17, open MicLink to resume.

If the app shows a Stay connected while paused switch, leave it on. It is what keeps the PC connected while MicLink is paused in the background.

7. Free time and MicLink Pro

Every feature works on the free plan. What you need for it is microphone time:

  • Get an hour: tap Start, or the + 1 hour button under the app's header, and choose Watch an ad. An ad watched to the end gives you 1 hour of microphone time. You can watch ads ahead of time and save up to 4 hours.
  • When it counts: free time is only used while you are streaming to your PC. Muted time counts too, because Mute keeps the microphone on and the stream running. Paused, or waiting for the PC, it stands still, so use Pause to stop the clock.
  • When it runs out: MicLink tells you a few minutes before. Then the microphone pauses and your PC stays connected. Watch another ad, or go Pro, and carry on. There is nothing to do on the PC.
  • Ads need the internet: the iPhone needs Wi-Fi or mobile data to load an ad. Your audio still goes only to your own PC.

MicLink Pro gives you unlimited microphone time and no ads, for $14.99 per year or $2.99 per month (US prices; they may differ in other countries). You subscribe inside the iPhone app, through Apple. The subscription renews until you cancel it in your iPhone's Settings › your name › Subscriptions; in MicLink, the three dots at the top right › Manage subscription takes you there. See pricing and the subscription terms.

8. Troubleshooting

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

The installer is not code-signed yet, so Microsoft Defender SmartScreen may stop it the first time. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Only do this for the installer you downloaded from this website.

Wi-Fi: my iPhone is not in the list
  • Check that both devices are on the same network, and that it is not a guest network.
  • On the iPhone, MicLink must be open, set to Wi-Fi, with Start pressed.
  • On the iPhone, check Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network and make sure MicLink is switched on.
  • On the PC, allow MicLink through Windows Firewall on private networks.
  • Or skip the list: type the address the iPhone shows into MicLink on the PC.
USB: the PC does not find my iPhone
  • Use a cable that carries data. Some cables only charge.
  • Unlock the iPhone and tap Trust if it asks.
  • Cable mode needs Apple's USB support on the PC. Run the MicLink installer again and let it add it, or install Apple's Apple Devices app from the Microsoft Store. Then unplug the iPhone and plug it back in.
USB: the iPhone is found, but it will not connect

Open MicLink on the iPhone, set Connection to USB and tap Start. Then press Connect on the PC again.

Connected, but the other app hears nothing
  • In the other app, the microphone must be CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable).
  • In MicLink on the PC, the output device must be CABLE Input.
  • Make sure you are not muted or paused on the iPhone, and that Mute PC is off on the PC.
  • The level meter in MicLink on the PC should move when you talk. If it does, the audio is arriving and the setting to fix is in the other app.
"CABLE Output" is missing from the microphone list

VB-CABLE is not installed or Windows has not loaded it yet. Restart the PC. If it is still missing, run the MicLink installer again while connected to the internet. When Windows asks Would you like to install this device software? for VB-Audio's driver, choose Install: without it there is no CABLE Output. Then press Rescan in MicLink.

Crackling or short drop-outs

Raise the Jitter buffer in MicLink on the PC. On Wi-Fi, move closer to the router, or switch to the USB cable.

My voice arrives late

Lower the Jitter buffer in MicLink on the PC, and use the USB cable if you can.

People say I am too quiet or too loud

Move the Gain slider in the iPhone app. It goes from 0 to 200 %.

The lock screen says "Open MicLink to resume"

iOS did not let the microphone start from the lock screen. Open MicLink and tap Resume there. Your PC is still connected.

On iOS 17 this is expected: resuming from the lock screen or the Dynamic Island needs iOS 18 or later.

MicLink paused by itself

On the free plan, the microphone pauses when your free time runs out, and the lock screen says Free time used up. Your PC is still connected. Open MicLink, tap Add free time or get Pro, watch an ad for another hour or go Pro, and carry on.

A phone call or another app that takes the microphone also pauses MicLink. Tap Resume when you are done.

The ad does not load
  • Ads need an internet connection on the iPhone. With a USB cable and no Wi-Fi, switch on mobile data.
  • Wait a moment and try again. Sometimes no ad is available straight away.
  • An ad blocker, a VPN or a private DNS service on the iPhone can stop ads from loading.
I subscribed to MicLink Pro, but the app shows the free plan

Check that the iPhone is signed in to the Apple Account you subscribed with. Then tap the three dots at the top right of MicLink and choose Restore Purchases.

There are no MicLink controls on my lock screen

On the iPhone, open Settings, find MicLink and switch on Live Activities. The controls appear while a session is running.
